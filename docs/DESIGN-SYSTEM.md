# Visha Shop design system

**Brand:** Visha Shop — independent technology buying intelligence for India.

## Visual direction

The public storefront uses an original editorial-publication layout inspired by premium business magazines. It borrows the information hierarchy of a strong lead story, a latest-research rail, topic sections and deeper feature stories; it does not reuse Fortune's logo, assets, copy or proprietary visual elements.

The interaction principle is adapted from the SRM Online Hackathon planning work: keep the journey in one clear environment, make each stage understandable, and present information before asking the user to act. For Visha Shop this becomes **Discover → Understand → Compare → Choose**.

## Foundations

- Paper background: #fbfaf7
- Main ink: #171717
- Editorial accent: #bd332b
- Link accent: #145b9d
- Rules and borders: #dedbd4
- Display headings: Georgia / Times New Roman serif stack.
- Body and interface: system-first sans-serif stack.
- Main content shell: 1320px maximum width with responsive side gutters.
- Editorial cards: square or restrained corners, thin rules, no heavy dashboard shadows.
- Original cover art: local CSS illustration; no third-party image or font request is required.

## Page composition

1. Publication bar: section identity, edition and editorial-standard access.
2. Masthead: one clear positioning statement and a direct path into the buying finder.
3. Lead story: a real published guide, its research type, update date and reading time.
4. Latest rail: recent guides with category and reading metadata.
5. Topic index: category discovery backed by actual guide counts.
6. Editor's selection: featured guides from the content collection.
7. Decision method: Discover, Understand, Compare and Choose.
8. Transparency panel: visible affiliate status and editorial policy links.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Keep the desktop Explore menu, site search, mobile navigation and all guide/category routes functional.
- Use semantic headings, navigation landmarks, visible keyboard focus, descriptive link labels and reduced-motion handling.
- At narrow widths, collapse the lead story, latest rail and category grid into a readable single-column flow.
- The internal Research Desk remains a separate workspace; storefront publication styles must not bleed into AppLayout.

## Commercial and editorial safeguards

- Affiliate links remain disabled until the existing account, site, product and editorial approval gates pass.
- Do not fabricate live prices, stock, ratings, rankings, hands-on testing or customer reviews.
- Identify desk research as desk research. Only label a guide hands-on when documented testing supports that claim.
- Do not add analytics, external tracking, remote font dependencies or third-party product imagery as part of the visual refresh.
- Keep pre-release noindex and robots safeguards intact until the owner separately authorizes a public release.
